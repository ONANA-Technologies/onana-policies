Privacy Policy

Customers and vendors · Draft prepared 16 October 2026

Draft for implementation review. The operator must verify that the disclosures below match the released app, backend and service providers before publication. This draft is not confirmation that the described controls are already implemented.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

ONANA is a marketplace operated by Universe Advanced Technologies Limited, Kumbukumbu Street, Plot No. 73, Block 41, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. We are responsible for personal information processed to operate ONANA.

This policy covers the ONANA Android and iOS app and related ONANA website and services. It applies to customers browsing or contacting businesses and to vendors creating business profiles, publishing listings and managing their presence. The app combines customer and vendor functions; the information processed depends on the features you use.

For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@onana.co.tz, with “ONANA Privacy” in the subject.

2. Information we process

  • Account information: names, phone numbers, email addresses, account identifiers, login credentials and account preferences supplied when registering, signing in or updating a profile.
  • Vendor information and public listings: business name, category, contact details, business address or map position, descriptions, prices, listing photos or videos and other information a vendor submits for publication.
  • Verification information, where requested: business registration details and supporting documents submitted for vendor verification. Identity documents submitted privately for verification are not intended for public display.
  • Customer activity and submitted content: searches, viewed listings, saved items and enquiries; reviews, reports or in-app messages where those features are available and used. We also process information sent directly to our support team.
  • Location: a location you enter for searches or a business listing. If you grant device-location permission for a location feature, the app processes the location provided by your device to support nearby results or directions. The precision and scope must match the permission shown in the released app.
  • Device and service information: IP address, app and operating-system versions, request timestamps, security logs and technical error information generated when accessing the service. If notification, analytics or crash-reporting services are enabled, the associated tokens, usage events and diagnostics must be disclosed for the actual services in use.
  • Payment-related records, if a payment feature is enabled: the relevant purchase, promotion or subscription details, transaction reference and payment status. The payment provider processes payment credentials under its own privacy policy. The actual provider and any credentials received by ONANA must be specified before this section is published.

Do not upload another person's private information without permission, or include identity documents or confidential payment details in public listings.

3. How we use information

We use relevant information to create and manage accounts; display and search listings; help customers contact vendors and find locations; manage vendor profiles and verification; provide enabled messaging and notification features; respond to support enquiries; moderate content; investigate misuse and fraud; secure and maintain the service; and meet applicable recordkeeping and legal obligations.

Information collected for optional features is used for the purpose explained when that feature is enabled. Where consent is required, it must be obtained before the relevant collection or use. Optional marketing communications must provide a way to opt out.

4. Public information and contact with vendors

Vendor business profiles and published listings are visible to other users and may be accessible on the public web or through search engines. This can include the business contact details, address, map position and media submitted for publication. Public reviews or other public content, where supported, can also be seen by others.

When a customer contacts a vendor, information the customer sends becomes available to that vendor. If you choose Call, WhatsApp, Email or an external map service, the selected application or service handles that interaction under its own terms and privacy policy. Opening an external service does not mean ONANA receives the full contents of calls or messages made there.

5. Sharing information

We disclose information to the people you choose to communicate with and publish information you submit for public display. We also use service providers supporting hosting, storage, communications, security and other enabled technical functions. These providers receive the information needed for their role, subject to appropriate contractual and security requirements.

We may disclose relevant information when required by applicable law or a valid legal request, to investigate abuse or protect people and the service, or in connection with a business transfer subject to applicable safeguards and notices. We do not sell personal information.

If ONANA uses advertising or tracking services, their data collection, recipients and consent requirements must be separately disclosed before those services are enabled; a vendor listing alone does not establish what advertising data is collected.

6. Permissions and your choices

Camera or selected-photo access, where requested, supports taking or uploading profile and listing media. Location permission supports the location feature explained in the permission request. Notification permission supports alerts. You can manage these permissions through device settings; declining an optional permission may limit the related feature.

Permission prompts and this policy must reflect the app's actual behavior. Access to contacts, SMS, call logs, microphone or background location requires a specific disclosed purpose if implemented; it must not be assumed to be covered by general permission wording.

7. Security and storage

We use appropriate access controls and technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information. Personal information transmitted between the app and ONANA services must be protected using encrypted connections such as HTTPS. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Information may be processed where ONANA and its providers operate, including outside your country where international providers are used. We apply safeguards required by applicable data protection law to such transfers. The deployment review must confirm the actual storage and processing locations.

8. Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to operate the account and services, resolve outstanding enquiries or disputes, prevent fraud, protect security and meet applicable legal obligations. Retention depends on the type of record, its purpose, outstanding issues and applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymize it. Limited records may need to remain after account deletion for legal, security or dispute purposes; they must be restricted to those purposes. Backup copies are removed under the applicable backup lifecycle. Before publication, the operator must confirm its retention periods and backup deletion cycle and disclose any material exceptions here.

9. Account and personal-data deletion

Customers and vendors can request deletion of their ONANA account and associated personal information by emailing privacy@onana.co.tz with the subject ONANA Account Deletion. Include your registered email address or phone number and state whether the request concerns your customer profile, vendor profile or the entire combined account. Do not send your password.

We may verify that the account belongs to you before acting. Deletion of the entire combined account covers its customer and vendor profiles and associated personal information. Removing only a listing or vendor profile is different from deleting the whole account; we will clarify the scope if necessary.

Account deletion removes the account and associated personal information, subject to the limited retention grounds in Section 8. Public listings and profile information linked to the deleted account are removed from ONANA's active public service. Copies already obtained by other users or indexed by third-party search engines may remain outside our control.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. The released app must also provide a working in-app route to request account deletion; its exact location and processing timeline must be confirmed before publication.

10. Access, corrections and other privacy requests

You can update information using available account and listing controls, or contact us to request access, correction, deletion or information about processing. Where applicable law provides additional rights, you may request restriction, object to processing, withdraw consent or request a copy of your data. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully completed. We may need to verify identity and explain any lawful limits on a request.

11. Children

ONANA is a general marketplace and is not specifically designed for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in circumstances requiring parental consent without that consent, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Any minimum age or parental-consent requirements must be consistent with ONANA's terms and actual registration controls.

12. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when our practices change and show the effective date on this page. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent for material changes.
